# Contribution retenue — protocole prospectif

We propose a reproducible, layer-wise audit of frozen cell-graph neural networks that jointly measures concept decodability and prediction sensitivity under validity-screened interventions, with patient-grouped uncertainty and a locked cross-cohort evaluation in breast histopathology.

## Evidence the paper must produce

The contribution is not a saliency map or a class prediction alone. For every pre-specified clinical concept and frozen GNN layer, the paper must show: (1) held-out patient concept decodability; (2) an expected prediction change under a bounded, realistic intervention; (3) a target effect larger than matched control interventions; and (4) stability across patient splits, seeds, and the locked BACH cohort.

This is the empirical contribution: separating decodability, prediction sensitivity, and intervention validity. It remains a hypothesis until NuCLS, independent concept annotations, multi-seed experiments, and BACH evaluation have been completed.

Cette phrase décrit ce que le projet propose de réaliser. Elle ne revendique ni la première distinction entre encodage et utilisation, ni la première intervention conceptuelle en pathologie, ni un bénéfice empirique démontré. Le résultat scientifique reste à établir.
